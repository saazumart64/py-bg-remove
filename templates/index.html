Frequently asked questions

What to know before you create your cutout.

How do I remove a background?

Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP image, select Remove background, and wait for the result. The first request may take longer while the AI model starts.

Can I add my own background?

Yes. After processing, choose a color or select a background photo from your device. The photo stays in your browser and is not uploaded for background removal.

Which file types can I download?

A transparent result downloads as PNG. When you select a color or photo background, the combined image downloads as JPG.

Will every image have perfect edges?

AI results vary with image resolution, lighting, and how clearly the subject stands apart. Very fine hair, motion blur, or similar subject and background colors can need manual touch-up.
