About

Image editing, made simple.

{{ settings.logo_text or 'CleanCut' }} is a straightforward image tool for removing a background and preparing a cutout for your next project.

What CleanCut does

{{ settings.logo_text or 'CleanCut' }} uses an open-source AI image segmentation model to identify a prominent subject and create a transparent PNG. You can preview the result with a solid color or your own background photo, then download a transparent cutout or a flattened image.

It is designed for everyday product photos, portraits, and creative projects. Results depend on image quality and subject-background contrast, so we recommend reviewing each cutout before using it.

Our approach

We aim to keep the editor clear, accessible, and honest about what automated image processing can and cannot do. {{ settings.logo_text or 'CleanCut' }} does not promise perfect results for every image.
